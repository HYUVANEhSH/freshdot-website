What is FreshDot?

Freshness Meets Sustainability

FreshDot is an innovative biodegradable sticker that helps extend the freshness of fruits and vegetables by absorbing ethylene gas released during ripening. The sticker also changes colour to indicate when it is no longer effective and includes an interactive QR code with storage tips, recipes, and produce care advice. Unlike ordinary eco-friendly stickers, FreshDot actively reduces food waste while helping consumers live more sustainably.

Helping the Planet

Reducing Plastic & Food Waste

Every year, millions of tonnes of food are wasted because fruits and vegetables spoil too quickly. FreshDot helps tackle this problem by extending freshness while replacing traditional plastic stickers with biodegradable materials. One small sticker can make a meaningful difference to households and the environment.
